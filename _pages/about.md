## Purpose of this Project
- Describe the Climate Impact of Chemistry Research
- Understand how chemistry can produce CO2 emissions
- Explore mitigatin strategies to limit the footprint of research
- Study emissions from work in my research group

## What is a Changing Climate?

- As the concentration of greenhouse gasses in the stmosphere, driven by anthropogenic sources, the earths climate changes.
- Currently, the earth is experiencing a warming climate, which means average daily temperatures will increase.
- We are also experiencing a change in precipitation patterns, frequency and intensity of storms, and intensifying droughts.

## What Impacts can this Have?
- A warmer climate can excacerbate things like melting ice sheets.
- Increased drought conditions can lead to decreased farm productivity.
- Increased extreme heat events leads to more heat related deaths.
- Increased atmoshperic CO2 has also driven ocean acidity and ocean heating, damaging valuable ecosystems.
  
## How does Chemistry Research Contribute to a Changing Climate
- Chemical reactions can produce greenhouse gasses, especially CO2.
- Chemical research requires vast amounts of power for things like instruments, fumehoods, and producing consumables.
- Chemical waste, especially solvents, is typically combusted, emitting greenhouse gasses into the atmosphere.
