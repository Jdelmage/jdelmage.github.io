**Impact of Chemistry Research on the Earths Climate**

Before attempting to understand the climate impact of chemistry research in an academic setting,
it is important to contextualize this work. In the grand scheme of greenhouse gas emissions from 
the chemistry sector, academic research labs do not generate nearly as much emissions as 
industrial scale chemistry[2]. Data from 2016 estimates that the chemical industry was responsible for 
6% of global emissions, making it an important sector to address as governments aim for net zero[3].
However, for industry to transition to greener, more sustainable, chemistries, the reactions and 
precident needs to exist. Therefore, one of the roles that chemical research can play is in the design 
of chemistry that demands less resources and produces less emissions.
